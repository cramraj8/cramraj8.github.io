About Me

I am an Applied Scientist II at Amazon on the Query Understanding team, where I work on improving customer query understanding through brand prediction, product type prediction, and intent classification systems. I design, experiment, and deploy production-level machine learning systems, conducting A/B tests to validate impact at scale.

I hold a PhD in Computer Science from Emory University, where I was advised by Prof. Eugene Agichtein in the Information Retrieval Lab. My doctoral research focused on training search and ranking models with minimal supervision, spanning topics including domain adaptation, cross-lingual retrieval, neural ranking, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).

My research interests lie at the intersection of Information Retrieval, Large Language Models, and Vision-Language Models. I am passionate about building intelligent systems that bridge the gap between human information needs and machine understanding.
